Frequently Asked Questions

What are the school's operating hours?

We operate from Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Extracurricular activities run from 3:30 PM to 4:30 PM.

What documents are required for registration?

Parents must provide the child's birth certificate, 2 passport-size photos, and previous academic progress reports.

Is school transport available for students?

Yes, APAPEC-IREBERO offers safe bus transportation covering all major sectors in Kigali.
